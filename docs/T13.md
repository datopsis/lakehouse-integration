## Limits

Static S3 credentials remain private fixture scaffolding; named-table denials
are not arbitrary-path storage isolation. Phase D STS is not qualified. Sail
and direct-client machine identities have no grants on the row/mask table;
Trino's trusted connector identity must load its metadata. A trusted host or
holder of its broad static keys can bypass engine restrictions.

Revocation checks use fresh catalog requests/queries/Spark sessions and old
unexpired human/team tokens. They do not revoke fetched data, already admitted
queries, engine caches or previously issued storage credentials. A supervisor
obtains team tokens; the automatic token-broker role is still unimplemented.

Every evaluated authorization decision requires a committed audit. Transport
rejections before a policy request exists and failure of the audit database
cannot durably record themselves. Gateway decisions are exactly reconciled;
all fixture principals require both permit and deny records, exact row/mask
results are persisted, and the complete audit digest must survive DB restart.
C1–C8 retains the fresh-empty-human-group compatibility gap documented upstream.
